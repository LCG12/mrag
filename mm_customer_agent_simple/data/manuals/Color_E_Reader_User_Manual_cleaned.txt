Notes

1. If the player cannot power on for the first time, please charge it via USB cable until the battery indicator is full.
2. Please handle this reader with care to prevent screen damage.
3. Please do not remove the built-in battery when the device is in charging mode.
4. The built-in battery is replaceable; however, this needs to be done by the manufacturer. Please contact the manufacturer by phone or email.
5. Please contact the manufacturer if you encounter a problem with the device. Do not attempt to disassemble the device by yourself.

* Video: AVI, RMVB, MPEG2

Power Management

# Charging the battery

The e-book's battery has been partially charged at the factory. The battery indicator is located on the upper right-hand side of the Main menu when you turn on the device. The battery indicator is a graphic that shows the power left in the battery. If you find that the battery indicator has less than 2 bars, please charge the device via USB cable to an AC adaptor or to a computer. Please unplug the USB cable once the device is fully charged.

# Charging via USB data cable to the computer

1. Connect to your computer via USB data cable. Go to the "Safely Remove Hardware" icon located at the bottom of the Windows task bar, then left-click it. This will safely remove your USB mass storage device.
2. Now the device goes to USB charging mode, and you will see a charging battery icon displayed on your e-book reader's screen. Please note that if you charge the device via computer, it will normally take longer than via AC adaptor. Again, please do not remove the battery while charging is in process.

# Replacing the Battery

This device includes a Lithium polymer rechargeable battery (3.7 V, 2000 mAh). Should you need to replace the battery, please contact the manufacturer and do not attempt to do so by yourself. This process can only be done by the manufacturer’s technician.

General Operation

# Power ON/OFF the device

1. Slide the Power On/Off switch to ON, then press and hold the Power button for 5 seconds and release the button to turn on.
2. To turn OFF the device, press and hold the Power button for 5 seconds. We recommend sliding the Power On/Off switch to OFF if you do not plan to use this device for a certain time.

# Button operation

1. The Navigation button: Use the "<" and ">" keys to select the left/right/up/down direction.
2. Confirm/Enter: For selected items, press the "M" key to enter the currently selected function or refresh the screen.
3. ESC: Press the "Home/ESC" button to return to the previous screen.

# Main Menu

Document Management

# Connect e-book to the computer

Please connect this device to your computer via USB data cable.

This reader will automatically turn on and the screen will show “USB Connection”. Your computer will identify this reader as a Removable Disk after the connection is completed. Your computer will show 2 removable disks for this device: one is for the built-in internal memory of this device and the other is for the Micro SD card (not included), if you have inserted one.

# Manage files

After the device is properly connected to your computer, you can select the removable disk in "My Computer" and start to transfer, copy, or delete files between this device and your computer.

# Inserting a Micro SD card (not included)

You can expand the memory of this device (up to 16 GB) by inserting a micro SD or SDHC card (it is also called a TF card). First power off the device, then insert a micro SD card and turn the device back on.

# Note about the use of Micro SD card

If your device encounters a problem identifying your inserted Micro SD card, please turn off the device, pull the memory card out, and reinsert it again.

VERY IMPORTANT: Please make sure to turn the power off when pulling the memory card and/or inserting the memory card. Also, do not reset the unit when your memory card is inserted into the device.

# Taking Care of your E Reader

This E Reader is designed to be light, compact, and durable. However, since it is an electronic device, it must be treated and maintained carefully. Putting unnecessary pressure on it or striking the device against other objects can cause damage. In order to avoid damage to your E Reader, please:

* Keep the device covered when you are not using it. This will protect the screen from being scratched or damaged in any way.
* Do not drop, crush, bend, or apply excessive force onto the device.
* Do not expose this device to moisture, extreme temperature, or any harmful conditions.
* Clean the device by wiping with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use harsh chemicals.

# Maintenance and Attention

Please do not attempt to repair or disassemble this reader. Contact the manufacturer should your device encounter the following:

1. The device is subject to spills or foreign liquid objects falling into the device.
2. The device gets wet by rain or water.
3. The device gets damaged by falling.
4. There is obvious overheating.
5. After implementing the correct operation, the product still cannot work properly.

# Firmware Upgrade

We will upload any firmware onto our website if it needs any firmware upgrade in the future. You can also download the user manual and/or any other instructions related to this E Reader.

# The proper environment when using this reader

The appropriate temperature for this device is between 32 to 120 °F (0–50 °C). Beyond this temperature range, the screen display will deteriorate, so the user should avoid using it outdoors in the winter. Please do not place this device in environments with moisture, dust, or high temperature.

# Avoid causing extrusion of the device

Please do not shake, squeeze, or impact this device severely, and avoid imposing too much pressure on the screen and device. We recommend that you put the device in the leather case provided in the package for better protection.

Avoid plugging objects into the Device

Do not place any object in the gaps or other openings. They are used to provide ventilation and heat dissipation. These openings cannot be blocked or hidden.

# Troubleshooting

| No. | Problem | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cannot power on the e-book reader | a. Please charge the device until the power indicator bar is full.<br>b. Defective device; please contact the manufacturer for instruction. |
| 2 | Not able to charge the device | a. Defective AC adaptor; please contact the manufacturer for replacement.<br>b. Defective USB Cable; please contact the manufacturer for replacement. |
