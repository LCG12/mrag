# Washing Machine Owner's Instructions

【Manual39_0】

# Features

**Powerful washing by large pulsator:**

This washing machine has a big pulsator so that it can wash evenly.

**Three-way lint filter:**

The three-way lint filter strains off lint efficiently during washing, so you can wash laundry cleanly.

**Normal or soft selector:**

You can wash laundry efficiently by two types: Normal or Soft.

# Cautions

Be sure that the washer is grounded.

To avoid electrical shock, use a metal pipe for the ground connection. Do not ground the washer with gas pipes or telephone lines to avoid the dangers of explosion or lightning strike.

【Manual39_1】

Do not put your hands into the spin dryer basket during spinning.

【washing_machine1_02】

Your fingers can be caught by spinning laundry and damaged.

Never allow children to operate or play with the washer. Children playing with the washer may accidentally fall into the washing tub.

【washing_machine1_01】

Disconnect the power cord from the power supply after use.

Ventilation openings must not be obstructed by carpeting when the washing machine is installed on a carpeted floor.

If the supply cord of this appliance is damaged, it must only be replaced by an authorized technician appointed by the manufacturer, because special purpose tools are required.

Warning: to avoid overfilling and damage, this appliance must be filled with the operator present using a moderate water supply pressure only.

# Precautions When Operating

**Do not use excessively hot water.**

Plastic parts may be deformed or damaged. Clothing may also be deformed or discolored.

**Before washing, empty all pockets.**

If nails or pins remain in pockets, they may damage the washer or clothes.

**Close the water tap a little if the water pressure is too high.**

**Be sure to cover laundry with the inner cover before spinning.**

This prevents laundry from being tossed out and damaged.

**To avoid water splashes, close the wash tub lid.**

**Never splash water on the control panel.**

# Installation

**Environment:**

**Leave some space.**

Leave at least 15 cm between the washer and the wall.

**Place the washer on a sturdy, flat surface.**

If the washer is placed on an uneven or weak surface, noise or vibration may occur.

**Never install the washer near water.**

Do not place the washer in steamy rooms or where the washer is directly exposed to rain. Moisture may destroy the electrical insulation and cause an electrical shock hazard.

**Avoid direct sunlight or heating devices.**

As plastic and electrical components are affected by direct heat, never place the washer near heaters, boilers, etc. Do not place under direct sunlight.

Install the drain hose about 70-80 cm above the ground for pump model.

【Manual39_24】

**Connecting the drain hose:**

1. Before connecting the drain hose, remove the cover (a) from the drain hole.
2. After pressing the joint ring (b), insert the drain hose (c) to the drain direction.

【Manual39_25】

**Mounting the legs:**

Lay down the washer with its back facing downward. Insert four legs into the leg mounting sections of the base corners of the main body.

Insert the claw of the leg into the square hole of the leg mounting section, and push the leg in the arrow direction as shown in the figure until it clicks.

# Cleaning the Machine

**Cleaning the lint filter:**

If the dregs are filled up in the lint filter after finishing the wash, push down and pull it out as shown in the figure. Remove the collected lint and rinse the filter net.

【Manual39_27】

**Drain filter:**

In case of incomplete drain, disassemble the drain filter with a screwdriver (+), and remove lint from the filter as shown in the figure.

【Manual39_28】

**Overflow filter:**

Pull the upper side of the overflow filter with two fingers into the hole in the direction of the arrow as shown in the figure.

【Manual39_29】

**Cleaning the wash tub:**

First, fill water in the wash tub to the level indicated, then drain water from the wash tub while operating the wash cycle for 1-2 minutes. Close the wash tub lid.

**Cleaning the washer body:**

Wipe off stains on the cabinet and operation panel with a soft cloth. Do not use benzene, thinners, cleanser, or wax, and do not scrub the washer with a brush. Painted surfaces or plastic parts will be damaged.

# Maintenance

**When you load the laundry into the spin basket:**

Press the laundry evenly and put the safety cover into the spin basket drum as shown in the figure.

【Manual39_33】

**To pick out clothing which has fallen between the basket and tub:**

For safety, pull the power supply plug out of the socket.

1. Disassemble the screw from the spin dryer hatch.
2. The spin dryer hatch is fixed to the brim by the concave and convex parts at two places. Expand the concave and convex parts, and pull up the spin dryer hatch to remove it.
3. Remove the clothing.
4. Close the spin dryer hatch. Push in the hatch firmly until the catch at the front is engaged securely.
